# CS103 Student Survey · Google Forms specification

This questionnaire is administered in Google Forms. It must not be embedded, duplicated, or collect responses on the course website.

Published responder URL: [Open the CS103 student survey](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfLBvreTtqW7G-F_Ayocfei6kUX-cXsosVClhOrnNrMi2e7rw/viewform)

## Form settings

- Title: `CS103 · Starting-Point and Learning Experience Survey`
- Description: `There are no right backgrounds or preferences. Honest answers help the instructor choose examples, pacing, support, accessibility adjustments, and office-hour times. Responses are identified so the instructor can provide appropriate follow-up and support.`
- Collect verified email addresses.
- Restrict respondents to one signed-in response.
- Do not make questions required unless marked below.
- Keep response editing off and show the standard submission confirmation.
- Confirmation message: `Thank you. Your responses will help shape examples, pacing, support, and office hours for CS103.`
